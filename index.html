I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science and Data Analytics at Rhodes College in Memphis, TN. I received my Ph.D. in computer science from the University of Utah and my B.Sc. from Rhodes College.

Background

Before joining Rhodes, I spent a year and a half as a research engineer at Astera Institute in Emeryville, CA, on the Obelisk team, working at the intersection of deep reinforcement learning and neuroscience toward brain-like AGI for naturalistic 3D tasks.

During my Ph.D. at the Scientific Computing and Imaging Institute, I worked with Shireen Elhabian on statistical shape modeling, medical image analysis, and deep learning methods for building compact anatomical models from images—including work integrated into the open-source ShapeWorks package.

Teaching

In Fall 2026 I am teaching COMP 141 and COMP 322. See the teaching page for the full schedule.

Research Interests

Image processing, deep learning, statistical shape modeling, mathematical optimization, medical image analysis, and machine learning theory.
