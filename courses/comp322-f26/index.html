An introduction to computer vision, focused on image processing: digital image representation, enhancement, restoration, analysis, and segmentation, plus topics such as object detection, 3D reconstruction, and tracking. Applications include facial recognition, medical image analysis, robot navigation, and computational photography. See the official course description.

Course calendar

This schedule is tentative and will be updated as the semester proceeds. We meet Tuesday and Thursday; the first half covers core image-processing tools. After the opening lectures, we will spend time on linear algebra and Python review, moving faster or slower depending on class pace. After fall recess, topics will be chosen according to student interest—the field is broad (geometric transformations, feature matching, machine learning for vision, and more), and we will shape the second half as the semester unfolds.
